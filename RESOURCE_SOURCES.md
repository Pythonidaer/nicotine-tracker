# Quitting nicotine: source and content notes

Reviewed: 2026-10-19. Maintainer: this repository's owner. Review after a linked source changes, new pouch-cessation evidence appears, or product scope changes. These are original summaries and app-specific planning suggestions, not copied articles or an automated treatment plan.

| Source | Used for | Scope and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CDC: Nicotine Pouches](https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/nicotine-pouches/index.html) | Addiction, research gaps, no FDA approval as a smoking-cessation aid | Pouch-specific background; not a cessation trial |
| [Smokefree: Prepare to Quit](https://smokefree.gov/quit-smoking/getting-started/prepare-to-quit) | Planning and identifying triggers | Smoking guidance; app-specific examples are explicitly adaptations |
| [Health Canada: Smoking triggers and coping](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/publications/healthy-living/smoking-triggers-coping-101-infographic.html) | Five-minute delay, distraction, breathing, water, social support | Public-health smoking guidance; no claim that a pause is a proven pouch treatment |
| [Smokefree: How to Manage Cravings](https://smokefree.gov/challenges-when-quitting/cravings-triggers/how-manage-cravings) | Ordinary gum, water, activity and coping choices | Smoking guidance; no promised effect size or pouch-specific efficacy |
| [Smokefree: Managing Nicotine Withdrawal](https://smokefree.gov/challenges-when-quitting/withdrawal/managing-nicotine-withdrawal) | Symptoms, variation, when to seek professional support | Smoking cessation; no fixed pouch-withdrawal schedule |
| [Cochrane: Interventions for smokeless tobacco use cessation](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD015314_what-are-best-ways-help-people-stop-using-smokeless-tobacco) | Counseling, brief advice and varenicline probably help; NRT may help | Review published April 2025, evidence through February 16, 2024; smokeless tobacco is not equivalent to modern nicotine pouches |
| [FDA: Nicotine and replacement therapies](https://www.fda.gov/tobacco-products/health-effects-tobacco-use/nicotine-why-tobacco-products-are-addictive) | Distinguishing medicinal nicotine replacement from ordinary gum | Smoking-cessation context; no personalized drug choice or dose |
| [CDC: Calling a quitline](https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/quit-smoking/quitline/index.html) | U.S. quitline connection and coaching | Ask the service about pouch use and local eligibility; do not promise free medication |

## Content boundaries

- The resource is educational; it does not diagnose dependence, prescribe a taper, recommend a dose, or promise cessation.
- Pouch-specific evidence, smokeless-tobacco evidence and smoking guidance remain visibly distinguished at the point of use.
- Counts, labeled mg and purchases remain separate measures. Labeled content is not absorbed dose.
- Comparing logged days is descriptive, not causal proof. Unlogged days remain unknown.
- Craving/replacement tracking and reflections are still future features.
- Source links open externally and need a connection; the original guide is cached with the app for offline reading.
